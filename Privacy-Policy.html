Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application WorkHours · Log (hereinafter referred to as "the App"). We take your privacy seriously. This App is a fully local calculation tool. We do NOT collect, upload, or store any personal information on remote servers.

1. Data Collection

The App does not collect any personally identifiable information, including name, phone number, email, location, device identifiers, etc. All work hour records, date calculations, recurring reminders and custom holiday settings created inside the App are stored only locally on your iOS device.

All calculations and reminder scheduling run locally on your device. Your data will never be transmitted over the internet. The App contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK or crash reporting SDK. No user behavior tracking.

You can manually delete records inside the App, or uninstall the App. All local data on the device will be removed accordingly.

The App does not integrate any third-party services, analytics platforms or ad networks.

This App is a productivity tool suitable for all ages. We do not actively collect personal information of minors.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy. The updated version will be published on this GitHub page. Major changes will be highlighted on the page.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the contact information on the App Store listing.
